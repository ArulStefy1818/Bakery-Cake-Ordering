HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to some of the questions our customers ask most often.

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?

For custom celebration cakes, we recommend placing your order several days in advance. Larger or highly detailed designs may require additional preparation time.

Can I request a specific cake flavor?

Yes. You can contact our bakery team to discuss your preferred flavors, fillings, frosting and other cake requirements.

Do you make cakes for weddings and large events?

Yes. We create celebration cakes for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, corporate events and other special occasions.

Can I place a bulk bakery order?

Yes. For larger orders of cakes, pastries, cupcakes or bakery items, contact our team with your quantity and preferred date.

Do you offer delivery?

Delivery availability depends on the order, location and selected delivery date. Contact our team for current delivery options.
